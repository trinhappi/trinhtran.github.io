PROJECT 01

From Content Monitoring
to Competitive Intelligence

Question: What additional value could be created if competitor publications were analyzed not only as market information, but also as evidence of content strategy and thought leadership?

Competitor Monitoring ↓ Content Classification ↓ Structured Database ↓ Content Intelligence Analysis ↓ Strategic Insights
SSituation

My team, Market Intelligence, monitors competitor websites and maintains a market intelligence database. While following the content team's discussions in Group Marketing & Communication meetings, I noticed a gap: the team's knowledge of what competitors publish on their websites, and how, was limited and scattered.

TTask

This was a self-initiated project, beyond my role, as I work in Market Intelligence and not in the content team. My goals were to give the content team a consistent view of competitor publishing strategies, to contribute to the performance of Group Marketing & Communication, and to deepen my knowledge of content marketing and my data analysis skills.

AAction
  • Built a database as a calendar view in SharePoint Lists, so the team can see weekly what competitors publish at a glance.
  • Worked with content team members to apply our company's content taxonomy to competitor articles, so every competitor is analyzed through the same lens.
  • Extended the taxonomy with additional layers covering content format, packaging and editorial style, not only topics.
  • Solved the hardest part, classifying articles that fit no single category, by adding an extra field with defined levels so outliers are handled in a structured way. For example, evergreen (educational, thought-leadership) content sometimes embeds a small promotional part, so I added a commercial intensity level, from low to medium, for evergreen content.
  • Used Power Query and Power BI to build a dashboard tracking publishing cadence, trends, editorial styles and heatmaps of the most popular content, with historical trends available on demand.
  • Updated the database weekly, improved the dashboard continuously and compiled a quarterly report.
RResult
  • A database of around 350 articles from the 3 biggest players in the market (Sep 2025 to Sep 2026), a dashboard with 9 visualizations and a detailed taxonomy of categories and sub-categories.
  • After I introduced the project and the report, Group Marketing & Communication welcomed it and it became part of the quarterly reporting.
  • The content team uses the report as a reference for content production decisions and as a benchmark against the industry.
  • It saves the team from repeating the same research, and gives them historical data to look back on and a visual way to reflect on their own performance over time.
Power BI · Power Query · Excel · SharePoint Lists · Competitive Intelligence
PROJECT 02

From Workflow
to Data Model

A self-initiated project translating business workflows into a structured analytical model.

Question: How can complex business workflows be translated into a data model that enables meaningful analysis, supports decision-making, and delivers valuable insights for stakeholders?

Business Workflow ↓ Entity Identification ↓ Star Schema Design ↓ Data Transformation ↓ Dashboard Development ↓ Business Analysis
From Workflow to Structure — Overview dashboard built on a custom-designed dataset.
Understanding Lead Quality
Measuring Channel Performance
SSituation

During a work project on telemarketing and marketing ROI, I interviewed marketing teams from units around the world. I found that some teams lacked tracking and measurement of their lead generation campaigns, and that there was no group-level overview of how leads are generated, nurtured and converted into business value. Data was managed locally and reported to the group separately, in different formats and with different KPIs.

TTask

I set out to design a syndicated database that lets the group monitor the lead generation campaigns of its local units, with a dashboard available to the whole organisation to follow each unit.

The dataset is fictional and created by me. It shows the ideal situation in which such data is available, and it let me demonstrate my data skills and show what information I would ask for to run root-cause analysis and what insights I could provide.

AAction
  • Used what I learned from my work projects about the information marketing teams hold to decide what the model needed to capture.
  • Used AI to help design a star schema for B2B lead generation campaign performance. The fact is the campaign, and the dimensions describe it, for example campaign type and audience or segmentation.
  • Chose a star schema for scalability. When leads grow into the millions, campaigns become more complex and personas more nuanced, new dimensions can be added to capture more features and trends in the data.
  • Used Power Query to prepare the data and Power BI to analyse it and build the dashboard.
RResult
  • The dashboard gives marketing managers an overview of how efficient each campaign type is in a given market.
  • It shows which persona, segmentation and campaign type work best in which market.

Next step: I plan to add lead lifecycle data from the CRM, so the model can also show how well sales and marketing collaborate and how much effort marketing puts into qualifying, nurturing, scoring and assigning leads to sales.

Power BI · Data Modeling · Star Schema Design · Business Intelligence · Dashboard Design
PROJECT 03

Audience Intelligence

A communication project exploring how audience needs, behaviour and preferences can shape content and campaign decisions.

Question: How can audience insights be translated into more relevant content and communication touchpoints?

Audience Research ↓ Behaviour & Needs ↓ Content Strategy ↓ Campaign Execution ↓ Audience Engagement
Social Touch by Stories — Using student and alumni stories to make communication more relatable to prospective students.
Orange Communication — Combining brand identity with audience-relevant visual and content elements.
Diverse Content, Immersed Touch — Research and interviews informed campaign and event communication.
Vibrant Messages — Creating relatable touchpoints through activities, contests and youth-oriented communication.
Video Making — Expanding communication formats through student-focused video storytelling.
SSituation

I worked full time at a faculty, where I led a team of around 30 volunteer students. The faculty wanted to connect with its target audience, high school students and undergraduates, and to reach the highest enrollment rate of the academic year.

TTask

I was responsible for planning and budgeting the faculty's communication campaigns and for leading the volunteer team that carried them out, so that the faculty would build its reputation and stay connected with prospective students. I also stepped in on operational work, such as designing posters, writing video plots and handling event logistics.

AAction
  • Conducted audience research through interviews and online behaviour observation to identify needs, motivations and decision journeys.
  • Translated those insights into content themes, formats and communication approaches, from student and alumni stories to events, contests and video.
  • Analysed campaign feedback and engagement patterns to find improvement opportunities and adapt the approach.
  • Led the volunteer team through the hardest challenge: students' commitment was not stable, and some did not see why they were volunteering. I talked with each of them to understand their needs and direction, and explained clearly the team's goal and mission, with the pros and cons, so they could make an informed choice and commit more seriously.
  • Built a structured communication team that could be passed on to the next generations of volunteers, together with a culture of responsibility, positive support and enthusiasm.
RResult
  • Engagement on the faculty's Facebook page (comments, likes and shares) grew by 150%, and the faculty launched its TikTok account the same year.
  • Together with the team, I organised at least 20 successful events at the faculty, hundreds of Facebook posts and around 50 TikTok videos.
  • The faculty's reputation was built through these campaigns, and the communication team I structured and the culture around it continue with each new generation of volunteers.
Audience Research · Consumer Behaviour · Content Strategy · Social Media Communication · Campaign Development · Team Leadership
